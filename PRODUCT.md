# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

People in Jaipur (mainly Vaishali Nagar and west Jaipur) with a broken or slow laptop: students, home users, small offices and freelancers. Most arrive on a phone from Google search or Maps, often stressed, wanting three answers fast: can you fix my brand/problem, roughly what will it take, and how do I reach you right now.

## Product Purpose

The website for a walk-in laptop repair shop. Success is a phone call, a WhatsApp message, a doorstep-service booking, or a visit to the shop. The site replaces the business card and shop sign online.

## Positioning

A motherboard-level repair shop (chip-level work, not just part swaps) for every major brand, on the ground floor of Prestige Tower near Amrapali Circle, which also comes to the customer's door anywhere in Jaipur.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services shown on the shop signage and banners:
- Motherboard repair (headline specialism: "Motherboard Repair Specialist")
- Screen replacement, keyboard replacement, fan replacement, speaker replacement, touchpad replacement, panel and base replacement, hinge repair
- OS installation
- RAM and hard disk / SSD replacement and upgrade

Brands on the signage: Dell, HP, Lenovo, Asus, MSI, Apple (MacBook), Alienware.

Confirmed promises (user-confirmed 2026-10-15):
- Home / doorstep service in Jaipur
- Free diagnosis / quote
- Same-day repair for common jobs
- Warranty on repairs

Undecided: warranty length, prices, doorstep-service area limits and fees, data-recovery capability. Do not state numbers for these.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "ILS IT Laptop Solution" is the brand (logo on the card); "Laptop Service Center" is what the shop is called on its sign and Google listing. Use both: ILS IT Laptop Solution as the wordmark, Laptop Service Center as the descriptor.
- Language: English only.
- Card tagline: "Book Home Service".

## Evidence on Hand

- Business card and shop signage photographs on the Google listing (not downloaded; low resolution).
- No testimonials, review counts, customer numbers, years in business or prices. Never invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Contact in one tap from anywhere on the page: call, WhatsApp, directions.
2. Answer "can you fix my laptop?" by brand and by problem, in plain words.
3. Only promise what the shop has confirmed; no invented proof or prices.
4. Phone first: most visitors are on a mid-range Android phone on mobile data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Plain English for non-technical, sometimes non-native English readers. Large tap targets, high contrast, fast on slow mobile networks.
